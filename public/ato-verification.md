## Method

The engine implements the ATO NAT 1004 (Schedule 1) weekly coefficient formula:

1. Convert period earnings to weekly equivalent: `x = floor(earnings / divisor) + 0.99`
2. Locate the correct bracket where `x < limit`
3. Apply the formula: `weekly = a × x − b`
4. Round the weekly result to the nearest dollar
5. Scale back to the pay period

For monthly pay cycles, the ATO's 33-cent adjustment is applied before conversion (`earnings + 0.01` if the amount ends in exactly 33 cents), and the back-conversion uses `round(weeklyRounded × 13 / 3)`.

LITO (Low Income Tax Offset) is embedded in Scale 2 and Scale 5 coefficients — it is not calculated separately. Medicare levy is embedded in Scale 1 and Scale 2.
